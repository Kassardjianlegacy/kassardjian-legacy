Preamble
Notice to All Institutions
Let it be duly recorded, and received by all institutions of finance, ministries of state, and houses of commerce of whatever standing, that the House of Kassardjian does not conduct its affairs in the manner of the ordinary commercial operator. It does not seek permission from markets. It does not solicit the confidence of counterparties. It does not present itself in supplication before any institution of finance, however august. It presents itself as it has always presented itself — as a sovereign commercial house of the highest order, operating upon the principles of absolute capital command, unyielding executive design, and the ironclad governance of a dynasty that was built to outlast its contemporaries.

The enterprise deems it proper to set forth herein, in terms admitting of no ambiguity and brooking no misapprehension, the precise architecture of its two permanent and integrated pillars of power, the doctrine under which it operates, and the standing upon which it presents itself to any institution of the Republic of Ghana or of the international commercial order that may have occasion to deal with it. What follows is not a representation. It is a declaration.



The House has always outlasted its contemporaries. It deems it proper to record that it intends to continue doing so.
